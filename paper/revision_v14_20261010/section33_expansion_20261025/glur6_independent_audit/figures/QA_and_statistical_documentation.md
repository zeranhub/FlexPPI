# Figure review and reproducibility

The figure is an exploratory case review of 3H6G, not a claim of functional causation or a complete mechanism. Its four panels display all 383 exact-sequence positions, both dimer chains and their arithmetic mean, five consecutive trajectory blocks, and sensitivity to removal of the largest MD effects. No manuscript file was edited.

The input files are the three audited CSVs one directory above the figure. `figure_data_audit.json` records their SHA256 hashes; `plotted_source_data.csv` records every plotted number. The script only reads those tables. It does not re-read the trajectories, select intervals according to prediction quality, smooth the residue curves or change model outputs.

Panel a shows each of the 383 positions once for MD and Full. For each position the arithmetic mean of dimer chains A and B is calculated first, and then the same monomer RMSF is subtracted. Negative values mean a lower RMSF in the dimer. This curve is not a trajectory mean with an uncertainty band: its vertical coordinate is the difference between previously averaged 100 ns RMSF labels. All positions are retained and connected in sequence order. Positions are prepared-input sequence indices, not a claim about original public PDB numbering.

Panel b shows mean absolute error over the 383 positions for each of five models. Chain A, chain B and the A/B mean are separate definitions of the dimer target; A and B are not independent simulation replicas. Full and Intra are the existing held-out record predictions from original fold 2. AA + SASA + contacts and Static47 + SASA are the corresponding existing fold-matched strong baseline predictions; zero change always predicts zero difference. No seeds or independently trained models are averaged here.

Panel c evaluates the same fixed state-difference predictions against labels recomputed separately for each of five consecutive 100 ns blocks. Each plotted value is an MAE over 383 positions using the A/B mean target. A single 500 ns trajectory exists for each state; the five time blocks are dependent observations from those trajectories, and the figure explicitly labels them as such. No test, P value, error bar, confidence interval, replicate count or block bootstrap is used.

Panel d removes the largest absolute MD differences, ranking on the true A/B-mean difference and applying the same retained positions to every model. Removal fractions 0%, 5%, 10% and 20% retain 383, 363, 344 and 306 positions. Removal rounds upward to the next whole position. The curves show MAE on the retained positions, without refitting any model. The ranking uses the observed case truth and is a sensitivity check rather than a deployable predictor.

Academic Figure Skill production assets were inspected for every panel: LineTrend/plot_sweep.py and its preview inform line widths, marker-based differentiation, tick sparsity and open spines; BarComparison/plot_comparison_Trajectory.py and its preview inform grouped model ordering. The existing assets' numerical data and bar geometry do not match this case; the figure therefore explicitly uses parameter inheritance, not native execution of unrelated example data. The required typography, palette and export baselines are included unmodified in the script, followed by the authorized task-specific Arial/DejaVu Sans and 8 pt or larger styling (time-block tick labels are 7.6 pt).

Quality review: blue, orange and greys are used consistently for models across panels, with distinct markers and line styles so colour is not the only identifier. No default multicolour palette, four-sided borders, chart bars, significance marks or smoothing are used. All four panels contain visible information and all text remains within the fixed 7.2 × 6.25 inch canvas. An initial header/title collision was corrected by increasing header spacing, then the PNG was re-rendered and visually inspected. The final PNG has readable labels and no clipped or overlapping text, and legends sit outside the data areas. The script checks per-panel non-background pixel density, verifies embedded TrueType fonts in the PDF and editable text in the SVG, and asserts that every expected record is present. PDF and SVG remain vector; PNG is 2160 × 1875 pixels at 300 dpi.

Verdict: ready for user review. This review figure intentionally does not display statistical significance, functional interpretations or a mechanistic superiority claim. The independent case audit documents additional boundaries, including strong SASA performance at the direct interface and limited improvement after removing the whole-chain mean shift.
