# Figure caption

**Partner-associated redistribution of local motion in peptide-bound calmodulin.** **a,** CaM structures bound to CaMKKα, eNOS, nNOS and smMLCK peptides (PDB 1CKK, 1NIW, 2O60 and 1CDL). CaM is grey, peptides use partner colours, and selected CaM side chains are red. Deposited SeMet atoms in 1NIW are retained. **b,** L39, L105 and M144 environments in the nNOS/smMLCK comparison, with local backbone alignment and a shared camera for each pair. First deposited models provide static localization, not a representation of motion; structure and NMR peptide constructs differ at their termini. **c,d,** Experimental S² heatmaps for every unambiguous probe shared by all four states: 87 backbone N–H probes at 87 residues (c) and 44 methyl-axis probes at 34 residues (d), using a common 0–1 colour scale. **e,f,** N–H S² at L18, L39, L105, M109 and M144 (e) and methyl-axis S² at L18-CD1, L39-CD2, L105-CD1, M109-CE and M144-CE (f), comparing nNOS (blue circles) with smMLCK (warm squares). Error bars are deposited fit errors, not confidence intervals or complete experimental uncertainties. At L39, N–H S² is 0.939 in both states while methyl-axis S² decreases from 0.779 to 0.329; at L105, N–H values are 0.972/0.967 while CD1 increases from 0.285 to 0.590. The second L105 methyl probe, CD2, increases from 0.256 to 0.551 and remains included in the full dataset, although not repeated in f. CD1 and CD2 are two probes of the same residue. **g,** All 44 methyl-axis differences (smMLCK − nNOS): 21 positive and 23 negative, despite similar means (0.5459 and 0.5486; rounded to 0.546/0.549 in the figure). The mean difference is +0.0027 and median absolute difference is 0.063. Selected sites illustrate the pattern; they are not a separate population sample.

Higher S² indicates more restricted fast local orientational motion. N–H and methyl-axis S² measure different observables and are not calibrated motion-amplitude equivalents or interchangeable with RMSF or Hχ. nNOS/smMLCK records match in temperature (308 K), pH (6.5), buffer and proton frequencies (500/600 MHz); CaMKKα differs in buffer concentration and frequencies. Similar means do not establish equivalence, and static contacts do not establish causation. No p values are calculated and probes are not biological replicates. This is an experimental-data figure, not validated FlexPPI output. Data: Frederick et al., *Nature* (2007), [doi:10.1038/nature05959](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature05959); BMRB 15183, 15185, 15191 and 15186.
