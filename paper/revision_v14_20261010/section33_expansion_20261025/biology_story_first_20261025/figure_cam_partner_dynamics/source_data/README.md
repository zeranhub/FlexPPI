# Figure data contract

All CSV files use UTF-8 with BOM (`utf-8-sig`) and retain original deposited S² values and fit errors without rounding. Partner order is `CaMKKalpha`, `eNOS`, `nNOS`, `smMLCK`; `CaMKKalpha` renames the earlier source-table column `CaMKK` without changing any value. Probe identity is observable + CaM entity sequence position + amino acid + atom name. All four CaM entity sequences match. Each table was independently checked against all four deposited NMR-STAR files; provenance and SHA256 hashes are in `../numeric_audit.json`.

`backbone_87_NH_wide.csv` and `sidechain_44_methyl_wide.csv` contain the exact four-way intersections with no ambiguous duplicate probe. Their `_long.csv` versions contain four records per probe, one per partner, with the associated BMRB ID. There are 87 N–H probes at 87 residues, and 44 methyl probes at 34 residues. A residue may have two methyl groups; counts are not biological replicates. The original nNOS Ile100-CG2 duplicate and nonstandard eNOS Thr29-CG5 never enter the common set and are not repaired or averaged.

The `_delta_smMLCK_minus_nNOS.csv` files retain both endpoint values, both fit errors, and `delta_smMLCK_minus_nNOS_s2`. Positive ΔS² means the corresponding fast local orientation is more restricted in the smMLCK state. `fit_error_sum` is only the sum of the two deposited fit errors for descriptive inspection; it is not an uncertainty or a confidence interval on ΔS². No error propagation, p value, hypothesis test, or equivalence test is used. A zero deposited fit error does not imply zero experimental uncertainty.

`key_sites_NH_and_methyl_wide.csv` contains 5 unique NH probes and 6 methyl probes: L18-CD1, L39-CD2, L105-CD1/CD2, M109-CE and M144-CE. The long version contains 44 rows. `key_sites_matched_NH_methyl_long.csv` contains 24 rows, one per key methyl probe and partner, with its corresponding NH value repeated; this repetition is for paired display and must not be counted as separate NH residues. Key sites are illustrative selections, while all shared probes remain in the whole-set panels. M109 is close to unchanged between nNOS and smMLCK and should not be described as a large effect.

For analyses that must match NH and methyl data at the same residues, use `NH_at_common_methyl_residues_wide.csv` (24 NH probes at 24 residues) and `methyl_with_corresponding_NH_wide.csv` (32 methyl probes at those same 24 residues). The full 87-NH and 44-methyl groups differ in residue membership. Do not interpret the difference in their spread as a calibrated ratio of motion amplitudes.

`descriptive_summary.csv` gives the exact means, ΔS² sign counts, mean ΔS², median absolute ΔS², and across-four-state median range for each scope. All 44 methyl probes: 21 positive and 23 negative differences, nNOS mean 0.5459090909, smMLCK mean 0.5486363636, mean difference +0.0027272727. Similar means are descriptive, not evidence of equivalence. `experimental_conditions.csv` gives conditions and source links; primary nNOS/smMLCK comparison matches conditions and frequencies better than the four-way comparison.

This figure contains experimental data and illustrative structures. It does not contain current or legacy FlexPPI predictions and does not establish model validation. S² is not Hχ, RMSF, configurational entropy, binding energy, affinity, or enzyme activity. Four peptide-bound states are compared; no apo/holo difference is calculated. Static structures locate residues and peptides but do not show their solution motion ranges or prove contact-mediated causation. See the captions and structural mapping tables for structure/construct limitations.
