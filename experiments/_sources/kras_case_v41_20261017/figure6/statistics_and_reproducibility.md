# Figure 6 statistics and reproducibility

Scientific claim: the same model recognizes the spatial pattern of peptide backbone-motion changes associated with the KRAS G6→D6 peptide substitution, including changes at the nine residues whose amino-acid identities are unchanged. The figure does not estimate affinity or binding free energy; its binding constants are source-literature measurements.

Panel a uses the actual structure files used as model inputs: 7OW5 WT and 7OW6 G12D. The sequence correspondence is positional and covers all ten residues: VVVGAGGVGK versus VVVGADGVGK. MHC Cα positions 1–180 define the rigid transform, with no outlier rejection or peptide refit. Both complex images share a camera and scale. The peptide-only enlargement uses the same transformed coordinates. All 10 peptide Cα atoms give RMSD 0.605462877 Å. Molecular images are PyMOL ray-traced renderings, with no artistic reconstruction. PyMOL rendering code, editable session, input paths and camera matrices are included.

Panel b has ten residue-level observations, not ten independent experiments. Its biological repeat unit is a complete MD trajectory: three existing 200 ns trajectories per state, six total. Each trajectory has 1001 stored frames. RMSF is calculated after independent chainwise Cα Kabsch alignment. Each state value is the arithmetic mean of three per-trajectory RMSFs. A difference is the G12D mean minus the WT mean. Error bars are sqrt(s_WT²/3 + s_G12D²/3), where each sample variance uses ddof=1; trajectories across the two states are not paired. Frames are not counted as independent repeats. No significance test or multiple-comparison correction is used.

The plotted model is family_full_no_ITrace, checkpoint SHA256 ff2974406d5d1ee07e6dc7118e43e57c0acc9849cc8feba7f5290476fca15ec5. This is the same joint RMSF/Hχ model used for the previous main case. All ITrace entries were held out in this family fold, with training based on ATLAS/DynaRepo. One selected, pre-existing checkpoint is used, without model refitting, new MD or selection of a different model for this case. The old strict model is not used in the plotted data or the reported primary scores.

Spearman ρ is Pearson correlation of the two sets of ranks over the specified residue set. Change MAE is mean(abs(ΔMD − Δmodel)), in Å. The no-change baseline is mean(abs(ΔMD)). Its MAE reduction is 1 − MAE_model/MAE_zero. The whole-peptide result is ρ = 0.806060606, MAE = 0.228881862 Å, zero-change MAE = 0.374770143 Å, MAE reduction = 38.9274%, direction agreement = 9/10. After removing only the chemically substituted position 6, the complete unchanged set is 9 residues: ρ = 0.733333333, MAE = 0.181509349 Å, zero-change MAE = 0.303332853 Å, MAE reduction = 40.1617%, direction agreement = 8/9. Both plots and primary scores use the same model. No positions are omitted for poor agreement. In particular, V2 is retained even though its predicted sign is positive while the MD mean change is negative.

Every visible quantitative point is in source_all10_peptide_model_and_MD_changes.csv, and all 60 underlying trajectory-level peptide RMSFs are in source_all10_peptide_six_MD_replicas.csv. These are extracted from the previously rechecked full-protein labels. plot_case.py checks all means, sample variances, difference standard errors, sequence identities, counts and correlations before plotting. figure6_data_audit.json records the source CSV hash, model choice, numeric scores and visible text. The six raw MD provenance and trajectory hashes are recorded in the original replica_recheck/7ow5/replica_label_audit.json and corresponding 7ow6 audit.
