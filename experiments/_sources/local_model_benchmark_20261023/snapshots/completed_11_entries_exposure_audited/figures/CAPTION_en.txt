Figure. Protein flexibility prediction across single-chain proteins, other multichain systems and TCR–pMHC complexes.
(a) Record-mean Spearman rank correlation; (b) average precision for high-RMSF residues, defined by reference RMSF at or above each record's 90th percentile, retaining ties; (c) RMSF mean absolute error in Angstroms for methods with physical amplitude outputs. All completed entries use the same 3,063 records and frozen valid residue identities; each record contributes equally. Colors distinguish FlexPPI configurations (blue), published learned models (ochre) and graph/simple controls (teal). Shapes identify complete-structure (circles), independent/within-chain (diamonds) and sequence (triangles) configurations, with FlexPPI highlighted by a square. Assemblies and independent-chain predictions are listed separately when tested. Intervals are 95% record-weighted family-cluster bootstrap intervals (2,000 resamples); the single-family ITrace stratum has descriptive points without an independent-family interval. Relative flexibility scores are not calibrated to Angstroms. Full, Intra, Static and the residue-type mean reuse frozen five-fold out-of-fold predictions, whereas external pretrained weights follow the documented inference protocols; training exposure differs and documented training-list ID matches exist. Full/Intra use record/biological-group OOF, with related-family training exposure; this is not sequence-family-held-out evaluation. The exposure audit and published-ID secondary analysis are reported separately. The reference removes chain-wise rigid-body motion, which may differ from external training targets. Only full-cohort completed entries are plotted; missing and unfinished outputs are disclosed in the coverage audit. The companion empirical cumulative distributions include all records without downsampling.
