Figure. Flexibility prediction for proteins and protein complexes. (a–c) Proteins (n = 2,203); (d–f) protein complexes (n = 860). Columns show record-mean Spearman rank correlation, average precision for high-RMSF residues (reference RMSF at or above each record's 90th percentile, retaining ties), and RMSF mean absolute error in Angstroms. All 14 completed method/configuration entries use the same 3,063 records and 961,321 frozen valid RMSF positions, with equal record weights. Homomultimers and split domains of one mature protein are proteins; distinct proteins or independent heterologous peptide partners are protein complexes. Classification was frozen from sequence-matched official annotations before binary-group performance aggregation. Blue, ochre and teal distinguish FlexPPI configurations, published prediction models and simple/graph controls, respectively; marker shapes and direct labels identify configurations. Intervals are 95% record-weighted family-cluster bootstrap intervals (2,000 resamples). The 244 TCR–pMHC records remain one dependency family within the protein-complex group. ANM, GNM and FastProtFlex produce relative scores and are not assigned physical MAE values. Full, Intra, Static and AA reuse frozen five-fold out-of-fold predictions; training exposure differs between methods and related-family exposure remains. The reference removes chain-wise rigid-body motion. Only completed full-cohort entries are plotted, and heavier models are deferred. The companion empirical cumulative distributions include all records without downsampling.
