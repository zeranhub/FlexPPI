max_drop_overview
Panel a: Expected functional hits among 30 candidates selected without functional-label calibration. Pool-random bar spans the exact hypergeometric 10th to 90th percentiles; grey tick is its mean. Position-matched random expectation holds fixed the max-drop rule's selected mutation-position counts, randomizing amino-acid choices within each position. Panel b: Additional hits versus pool-random expectation for five predeclared zero-label rankings; all methods select 30. Exact score ties have exchangeable expected membership, so comparator counts can be fractional. Each context includes all 171 nonparent single mutants. A functional hit is a highest-dose normalized CD8 4-1BB response ≥ 0.3; this endpoint is not binding affinity. TCR1*: construct-sequence discrepancy remains unresolved. CMV: NLVPMVATV; gp100: IMDQVPFSV; GRL: GRLKALCQR.

max_drop_all_contexts
Each point is one of all 171 nonparent single mutants in a fixed TCR context. The score is the largest signed predicted RMSF decrease, parent minus mutant, among the nine peptide residues on the same parent backbone. Blue highlights the predeclared top 30; grey shows the remaining 141. Dashed lines mark zero decrease and functional-hit threshold 0.3. Spearman correlation and AUROC use all 171 candidates; no response-dependent score direction or site exclusion was chosen. Every measured response is shown without clipping. This is an exploratory retrospective functional-response test, not direct affinity validation. TCR1*: construct-sequence discrepancy remains unresolved.
