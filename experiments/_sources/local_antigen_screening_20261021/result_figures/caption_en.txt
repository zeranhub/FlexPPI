RMSF and Hχ do not provide a stable joint gain in the fixed-backbone antigen pilot. (a,b) All 13 primary single-substitution candidates in each of the 1G4–NY-ESO-1 and A6–Tax HLA-A*02:01 systems (26 biological candidates in total). Each measured SPR ΔpKD is paired with a prediction from sequence plus static structural features (open circle) and the same baseline augmented with R and H (filled square); grey segments connect predictions of the same candidate. R and H are candidate-minus-parent mean predicted RMSF and Hχ changes at the prespecified, chemically unchanged environmental residues. All predictions hold out the entire mutation position within the corresponding system; no training fitted predictions are shown. The two panels use identical axis ranges, and the dashed diagonal is the identity line, not a fitted trend. Higher ΔpKD means stronger TCR–pMHC binding relative to the common panel parent. (c) Candidate-level out-of-fold mean absolute error for every one of the seven prespecified methods, using the reported-hybrid SPR label and the primary templates 2BNQ and 1AO7. Each point summarizes 13 candidates, rather than independent experimental replicates. Baseline and joint rows are shaded only to identify the comparison of interest. The zero-change method is shown by its MAE alone; no arbitrary tie-resolved ranking is inferred from its constant predictions. (d) All 15 combinations of the three predeclared SPR fitting choices and five predeclared templates, retaining the same 13 candidates per system. Asterisks identify primary templates. Numbers are MAE(baseline) minus MAE(baseline + R + H): positive values indicate lower error after adding the two readouts, and negative values indicate higher error. A single symmetric colour scale is centred at zero. These cells are sensitivity analyses, not additional experimental observations. Primary MAE increases from 0.4324 to 0.4488 in 1G4 and from 0.5985 to 0.6474 in A6; only 2 of the 15 sensitivity cells are positive. The base predictor is the historical strict_full model trained jointly on 2431 ATLAS/DynaRepo records with no ITrace training, not the manuscript's 2430-record checkpoint. The backbone is fixed and no new MD is used. No significance test or confidence interval is shown. This is a retrospective pilot in two known, related HLA-A2 contexts; it does not establish prospective antigen design, T-cell activation, broad TCR generalization or a binding-free-energy mechanism.
