# Figure 2 statistical and data documentation

The figure combines three distinct tests of model capability: overall record-level error distributions (a,b), geometry-related improvement within a fixed amino-acid type (c,d), and paired differences between full-structure and intrachain models grouped by the actual input chain count (e,f). No new training, inference or MD is performed. All results are existing five-fold out-of-fold predictions for the same 3,063 records. Main predictions use one selected checkpoint per fold; the two deep-learning variants were separately trained. The full collection is ATLAS, DynaRepo and ITrace. Counts are records and residue types rather than independent protein-family repeats.

Panels a/b contain all four methods (Full, Intra, Static and AA), all 3,063 records per method, and both targets. This is 24,504 ECDF observations, 12,252 per panel. Every ECDF ordinate is its sorted record rank divided by 3,063. No record is trimmed, downsampled or smoothed. The 0–1 empirical cumulative proportion is paired with MAE in Å for RMSF and nats for Hχ. RMSF uses a symmetric-log x-axis with a 0.1 Å linear threshold; its entire high-error tail remains within the axis range. Hχ uses a linear error axis. The full per-record frozen metrics are in source_archive/fig2_all_3063_four_method_metrics.csv; their source hash is in data_audit.json. Original ECDF coordinates are also preserved and checked point for point in the plotting script.

Panels c/d use the complete paired support table and only the pre-specified Intra and Static contrasts. They contain 20 valid RMSF types × 2 contrasts = 40 values and 18 valid Hχ types × 2 contrasts = 36 values. The plotted amino-acid ordering is AGCSTVNDHILFPWYQEMRK, inherited from the former source figure. Amino acids are categorical; there are no lines implying continuity across types. A/G have no defined side-chain χ angle and hence have no Hχ observation; their category labels remain visible in grey. The missing values are not encoded as numeric zero. Both negative within-type gains (Hχ/P and Hχ/V against Intra) remain in panel d. Pro/Static's high positive Hχ gain also remains fully visible. Panel d's signed axis is linear inside ±10 percentage points and logarithmic outside that interval; this preserves low gains and the full high outlier without cutting the range.

For each residue type and each comparison, the frozen table first calculates MAE within each record. Full and comparator means are then calculated across the same records with an available value, with each record having equal weight. The plotted percentage is 100 × (mean comparator MAE − mean Full MAE) / mean comparator MAE. This is a ratio of paired-support means, not a mean of record-specific percentage changes. All plotted values are in source_type_paired_gains_all80_rows.csv, including the four A/G not-applicable rows for the two comparisons. The complete 560-row upstream paired table is archived, including all AA and other comparator/source strata; no rows are removed from that archive. AA remains an overall baseline in a/b; the optional third within-type comparator is not added to c/d.

Panels e/f include each of the 3,063 records once per target: 2,193 single-chain inputs, 422 two-chain inputs and 448 inputs containing three or more chains. The observation is the same record's Intra MAE minus Full MAE, with positive values indicating lower Full error. Dots retain all observations and have deterministic horizontal offsets; there is no subsampling or randomized data selection. Black diamonds show record-equal means. Boxes show Q1–Q3 and the median; whiskers reach the farthest observation inside 1.5 × IQR. Outliers are all present as individual dots even though the boxplot does not draw a duplicate outlier marker. Signed symmetric-log axes have linear thresholds 0.04 Å for RMSF and 0.003 nats for Hχ. Full positive and negative tails are retained. Within the single-chain group the two independently trained models have the same chain availability, so these paired differences are model differences rather than an added-partner-chain intervention.

No confidence intervals, significance tests or multiple-comparison corrections are used: every mark here is a descriptive summary or an existing record-level observation. No residue or trajectory frame is treated as an independent protein replicate. panel_statistics.csv supplies every per-method summary, paired-support type result and per-chain-group count/mean/median/range/positive-negative count. source_all_visible_points.csv contains all 30,706 plotted source observations: 24,504 ECDF points, 76 type-gain points and 6,126 paired chain-group points.

The older ranking box panels, amplitude/error scatter panels and absolute type-MAE panel leave the main figure. Their former observations remain archived without modification: source_archive/fig3_all_12252_scatter_points.csv, fig3_all_6126_full_metrics_and_reference.csv, fig3_full_amino_acid_source.csv and fig3_amino_acid_paired_source.csv. No supplementary figure number is introduced by this replot.
