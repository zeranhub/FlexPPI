## Summary of all searched results

### 1. Overview

The two database queries returned 26 raw records, with duplicates across sources and two low-relevance arXiv matches. The high-signal manually verified corpus shows a coherent foundation for FlexPPI: Siamese paired encoders and antisymmetry from protein mutation-effect prediction, geometric/triangle interaction modules from PPI contact prediction, and residual Δ-learning from molecular AI.

### 2. Trends

From 2021 onward, protein interaction modeling shifted from generic GCNs toward E(3)/SE(3)-equivariant message passing, partner-specific geometric attention, and explicit residue-pair representations. Mutation-effect models independently converged on shared or Siamese encoders, embedding subtraction, forward/reverse consistency, and interface-local graph construction. Recent Graphinity results also emphasize that architecture does not remove the need for much larger or synthetic paired datasets.

### 3. Key themes

1. **Equivariant PPI geometry:** E(Q)AGNN-PPIS, EDG-PPIS, ASCE-PPIS and EquiDock use geometric symmetry as a built-in constraint.
2. **Partner-specific interaction attention:** DeepInteract, DeepInter and cross-attention affinity models explicitly represent inter-chain residue pairs.
3. **Paired Δ prediction:** GeoPPI, DDAffinity, DDMut and Graphinity encode two related structures and predict a signed change rather than two unrelated absolute values.
4. **Small-data robustness:** GeoPPI combines pretrained representations with a tree model; Graphinity demonstrates overfitting on hundreds of experimental samples and gains from large synthetic corpora.
5. **Residual or multi-fidelity learning:** Δ-machine learning establishes the general AI4S pattern of learning a correction to an existing lower-cost predictor.

### 7. Recommendations for reading

1. **GeoPPI (2021):** the closest early precedent for pretrained geometric representations of paired PPI structures followed by change prediction.
2. **DeepInteract (2022):** establishes partner-specific geometric interaction modeling that can replace an unconstrained late-fusion MLP.
3. **DDMut (2023):** provides a direct precedent for Siamese prediction with forward/reverse antisymmetry.
4. **DDAffinity (2024):** most directly connects shared structural encoding, IPA-related mutation models and PPI Δ prediction.
5. **Graphinity (2025):** supplies both the closest Siamese-EGNN architecture and the strongest warning that small paired datasets require pretraining or synthetic supervision.

## 5. Reading and benchmark recommendations

1. **PEGASUS** is the most useful sequence-only absolute-RMSF baseline and has a public Docker workflow.
2. **RMSF-net** is an important static-structure baseline only through its PDB-only branch; its full model has extra cryo-EM information.
3. **DynaProt** predicts a richer displacement covariance object from which RMSF is derived, but a complete official reproduction needs to be confirmed before inclusion in a frozen benchmark.
4. **Flexibility-Conditioned Protein Structure Design** belongs in a future design/generation track and must not be mixed with direct per-residue RMSF regression.
