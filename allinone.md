## Summary of all searched results

### 1. Overview

The two database queries returned 26 raw records, with duplicates across sources and two low-relevance arXiv matches. The high-signal manually verified corpus shows a coherent foundation for FlexPPI: Siamese paired encoders and antisymmetry from protein mutation-effect prediction, geometric/triangle interaction modules from PPI contact prediction, and residual Δ-learning from molecular AI.

### 2. Trends

From 2021 onward, protein interaction modeling shifted from generic GCNs toward E(3)/SE(3)-equivariant message passing, partner-specific geometric attention, and explicit residue-pair representations. Mutation-effect models independently converged on shared or Siamese encoders, embedding subtraction, forward/reverse consistency, and interface-local graph construction. Recent Graphinity results also emphasize that architecture does not remove the need for much larger or synthetic paired datasets.

### 3. Key themes

1. **Equivariant PPI geometry:** E(Q)AGNN-PPIS, EDG-PPIS, ASCE-PPIS and EquiDock use geometric symmetry as a built-in constraint.
2. **Partner-specific interaction attention:** DeepInteract, DeepInter and cross-attention affinity models explicitly represent inter-chain residue pairs.
3. **Paired Δ prediction:** GeoPPI, DDAffinity, DDMut and Graphinity encode two related structures and predict a signed change rather than two unrelated absolute values.
4. **Small-data robustness:** GeoPPI combines pretrained representations with a tree model; Graphinity demonstrates overfitting on hundreds of experimental samples and gains from large synthetic corpora.
5. **Residual or multi-fidelity learning:** Δ-machine learning establishes the general AI4S pattern of learning a correction to an existing lower-cost predictor.

## 1. What “geometric Transformer” means

The term is not a single model family. In practice it covers five architectural routes. An **invariant** model makes a prediction that stays numerically unchanged when the whole input is rotated or translated; for example, rotating a molecule by 90 degrees should not change its predicted energy. An **equivariant** model lets directional internal features and outputs rotate together with the input; for example, after rotating the molecule by 90 degrees, every predicted force arrow should also rotate by 90 degrees. Distance/angle-biased Transformers usually implement the former; SE(3)/E(3)/SO(3) tensor Transformers implement the latter by storing not only ordinary scalar features but also vectors and higher-order directional features. A third route introduces local coordinate frames or gauges and transports information between them. A fourth route represents geometric objects as multivectors in Clifford/projective/conformal geometric algebra. A fifth route is mainly concerned with spatial scalability: local windows, voxels, octrees, superpoints, serialized neighborhoods, or periodic crystal graphs keep attention computationally manageable.

## 5. Recommended reading order for FlexPPI

For a protein-protein-interaction architecture, start with **SE(3)-Transformer → Equiformer → EquiformerV2 → GeoMFormer** to understand tensor equivariance and the invariant/equivariant dual-stream design. Then read **AlphaFold2 IPA → DeepInteract → PeSTo → GET → GeoDock/DProQA** to see how geometric attention is adapted to residue-, atom-, and interface-level protein tasks. Finally compare **GATr** and **Gauge/Frame Averaging** against the tensor route, because these provide different ways to encode orientation without relying on exactly the same spherical-harmonic machinery. If computational scale is a central constraint, read **Point Transformer V3, OctFormer, Flash3D, E2Former, and the fast/global Clebsch-Gordan Transformer** as efficiency references.

## Summary of all searched results

### 1. Overview

The reproducible API rerun returned five Semantic Scholar records, of which PEGASUS, RMSF-net and DynaMode directly address MD-derived dynamics; the atom-level GNN predicts crystallographic B-factors, and A-RFP is a homolog-assisted docking method. Primary-source verification added modern direct predictors, physics baselines and ensemble generators needed to build a balanced FlexPPI benchmark.

### 2. Trends

The field has shifted from sequence-based B-factor or flexibility-class prediction toward direct MD-derived RMSF regression (Flexpert, PEGASUS, BackFlip), then toward richer covariance or trajectory prediction (DynaProt, DynaMode). At the same time, classical ANM/GNM and CABS-flex remain essential controls because they separate learned priors from static mechanical topology or explicit coarse-grained sampling.

### 3. Key themes

1. **Direct fast RMSF regression:** BackFlip, Flexpert, PEGASUS and RMSF-net produce residue-level continuous predictions.
2. **Complex-context mechanics:** ANM/GNM and CABS-flex can represent inter-chain contacts without learning from ITrace.
3. **Richer dynamics objects:** DynaProt predicts covariance and DynaMode predicts a time-resolved trajectory rather than one scalar per residue.
4. **Sequence-only controls:** PEGASUS and Flexpert-Seq measure what can be recovered without the experimental complex geometry.
5. **Ensemble generation:** BioEmu and AlphaFlow can yield RMSF after sampling but are monomer-oriented and substantially more expensive.

### Impact-aware experimental recommendation

The present FastProtFlex-only comparison is vulnerable because FastProtFlex, although now peer reviewed in *Bioinformatics*, is too recent to demonstrate that FlexPPI has been tested against established community standards. ### Synthesis

The search supports a signed redistribution formulation rather than a monotone contact-rigidity prior. The strongest immediate data path is to use existing DynaBench complex MD for interface-weighted absolute-state correction while keeping ITrace out of training; the strongest causal path is to add matched simulations that start from the same bound coordinates and differ only by whether the opposing biological component is retained. PINDER should define interface-level non-redundant splits and supply apo/holo structures, while DynaPIN annotations should stratify core, rim, support, persistent, and transient contacts. DynaPPI is promising but should remain auxiliary because binding-path trajectories and equilibrium per-residue fluctuations answer different questions.

### Recommended model: signed context-residual FlexPPI (working name: SCR-FlexPPI)

The recommended fine-tuning design treats the pretrained full-complex predictor as the carrier of the already validated global-flexibility ranking, rather than replacing it with a new interface model. For each biological recipient component, the frozen FlexPPI encoder is run twice on the same bound coordinates: once with the complete biological complex and once after removing only the opposing biological component. “Biological component” must be represented separately from PDB chain identity: for example, TCR alpha and beta remain together when pMHC is removed, and MHC, beta-2-microglobulin and peptide remain together when TCR is removed. The aligned recipient-residue representation difference, the frozen full-complex prediction, its uncertainty, the raw full-versus-removed prediction difference, and generic inter-component contact summaries feed a small shared residual adapter. The adapter predicts a signed correction for each target, and the final output is `frozen_full_prediction + interface_gate * signed_residual`. RMSF, side-chain entropy and SASA should have separate output heads because their physical response signs need not agree. TCR, MHC and CDR names should be evaluation strata, not model inputs, to avoid learning an ITrace-specific semantic shortcut.

This design is tightly coupled to the present code. FlexPPI already contains a shared encoder, a state head and a signed `ResponseHead`; the clean extension is a `forward_context_pair` path that reuses these modules and adds `component_index` plus a small residual head. The first training phase freezes the entire encoder and state head. If that underfits the interface response, the second phase may add LoRA or unfreeze only the final IPA/pair-update block, with replay and distillation; a full-backbone update should remain an ablation rather than the main method. There should be no isotonic or exact rank lock: that would preserve the current interface mistakes. Global capability is protected through frozen weights, strong non-interface distillation, replay if any backbone layer is opened, and a preregistered non-inferiority rule for full-complex macro Spearman, while interface residues remain free to reorder.

The training objective is `L = L_bound + alpha L_delta + beta L_sign + gamma L_noninterface_distill + eta L_replay`. `L_bound` is a heteroscedastic NLL or variance-aware Huber loss against full-complex MD labels. `L_delta` is allowed only for genuinely paired full/recipient-only MD and regresses the signed per-residue change. `L_sign` is evaluated and trained only when the paired change exceeds its replicate/bootstrap uncertainty; uncertain changes form a neutral class rather than being forced positive or negative. The distillation term is strong off-interface and weak or absent at the interface. Complexes and recipient sides, not residues, are sampled uniformly so that long multichain systems do not dominate. The physical target is a distributional redistribution of flexibility, not a forced 50/50 sign balance: negative interface changes can remain common, but near-universal negative predictions should disappear unless replicated MD supports them.

### Data plan

The immediate pilot can be performed without claiming physical partner-induced response. The current repository contains 210 DynaBench complex states and 39 test53 complex states with full-state labels, plus only 37 apo/holo pairs. These data can train an interface-weighted bound-state residual and test whether the frozen-plus-adapter architecture corrects full-complex interface RMSF/entropy/SASA without losing global rank. The existing ITrace cohort should not be used for training: it exposed the defect and therefore is best described as a targeted post-hoc stress test, not as a pristine prospective test. This pilot supports the claim “pretrained FlexPPI interface calibration,” but the full-versus-partner-removed model-input difference must not be labeled as a physical apo/holo delta because the coordinates and MD labels are unchanged.

The paper-grade causal version should construct matched counterfactual MD for approximately 80–120 interface-cluster-nonredundant PPI complexes, with a 24-complex subset used only as a feasibility pilot. For every bipartite complex AB, simulate AB, A-only and B-only from identically repaired bound coordinates, under the same force field, protonation, solvent, ionic strength and temperature, with three independent replicas and preferably 50–100 ns production per condition. Existing three-replica full-complex DynaBench trajectories can supply AB; the new compute then concentrates on A-only and B-only. Labels are calculated after recipient-core alignment as AB-minus-A for A residues and AB-minus-B for B residues, together with replicate variance. PINDER should define interface-structure-clustered train/validation/test partitions and help select systems; its structural apo/holo pairs are useful covariates and external checks, but are not themselves RMSF labels. DynaPIN annotations should stratify persistent/transient contacts and interface core/rim/support. Exact-PDB exclusion is insufficient: sequence, whole-structure and interface-geometry similarity checks should also be performed.

Formal sample-size claims should be made at the complex or recipient-side level. A fixed set of 24 complexes cannot guarantee adequate positive and negative changes, caliper matches and support in every `TCR/pMHC/CDR region x observable` cell. The primary study should therefore make generic PPI interface correction the estimand and reserve TCR/pMHC and CDR1/2-beta as biologically motivated external strata. If only 24 systems can be afforded, restrict the claim to one recipient direction and one well-converged observable such as backbone RMSF, or present the result as a feasibility study rather than a general response model.

### Architecture choices and decision

| Choice | Connection to pretraining | Main advantage | Main risk | Recommended role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Full end-to-end fine-tuning | Weakens the pretrained anchor | Simple | Catastrophic forgetting and interface shortcut learning | Baseline only |
| Last-block LoRA/adapters | Reuses most pretrained features | Cheap and expressive | Global and interface effects remain entangled | Second-stage baseline |
| Post-hoc scalar/isotonic calibration | Keeps the base score | Very cheap | Cannot repair residue ordering or signed mechanisms | Calibration baseline |
| Dual-pass signed residual adapter | Explicitly measures the pretrained model's context response | Best balance of continuity, attribution and safety | Requires careful component semantics; causal claim needs paired MD | Primary choice |
| Semantic role-gated mixture of experts | Can model TCR/pMHC asymmetry | High local capacity | Easy to overfit biological names and ITrace | Reject as primary; generic physical gates only |
| Separate intra-/inter-component attention channels | Clean mechanistic decomposition | Stronger causal control | Invasive and expensive | Phase 2 if residual adapter underfits |
| Ensemble/generative dynamics model | Represents multimodality | Biologically rich | Much larger compute and a different paper | Future work |

### Evaluation and falsification

The global primary endpoint is full-complex macro Spearman with a complex-bootstrap non-inferiority margin of `Delta rho >= -0.01`; report its confidence interval rather than only a point estimate. Interface endpoints are macro interface Spearman/MAE, signed-delta correlation, and macro balanced sign accuracy on reliable non-neutral paired labels. A direct over-rigidification audit should report the fraction of complexes whose contact responses are almost all negative and compare the predicted versus MD delta distributions. Results should be stratified by recipient side, core/rim/support, contact persistence and interface type, but only sufficiently supported strata receive confirmatory claims.

Required controls are the frozen pretrained model, full end-to-end fine-tuning, last-block LoRA, post-hoc calibration, a same-capacity residual head that sees only the full input, and the proposed adapter without the hidden-representation difference. A paired-target permutation control must shuffle MD deltas across training complexes while preserving base predictions and contact geometry; performance should collapse toward the frozen baseline. The method should be rejected if it fails to improve reliable signed-delta metrics, if the lower confidence bound for global rank change crosses the `-0.01` margin, if the shuffled-target control performs similarly, if gains disappear under interface-cluster split, or if side-chain entropy has not converged across replicas.

### Paper narrative and IdeaSpark audit outcome

The coherent paper chain is: state-only pretraining learns transferable residue flexibility; paired state fine-tuning teaches state response; the external multichain stress test reveals that full context improves global ranking while the pretrained context operator over-applies a contact-rigidity prior; SCR-FlexPPI freezes that global prior and learns only an uncertainty-aware signed context residual; matched counterfactual MD then tests whether the correction represents physical partner-induced redistribution rather than dataset calibration. This is a natural continuation of pretraining, not a separate downstream model.

The strict IdeaSpark audit did not approve an idea card after its one permitted retry. The first candidate failed because one partner-removed structure cannot supply both recipient directions and because a typed GNN plus rank-lock omitted simpler baselines and hid information loss. The second candidate replaced that design with the recommended frozen residual adapter and matched MD, but still failed the hard floor because 24 pre-split complexes cannot support broad region-by-observable claims, and because “counterfactual residual adaptation” alone is not a defensible novelty claim. Consequently, the method should be positioned around the protein-specific scientific finding, paired-MD benchmark construction, component-aware architecture and falsification evidence; it should not claim novelty merely from using a residual adapter.

# Clarified target class: soluble cytokines as dynamics-regulated PPIs

## Decision

The recommended **protein class** is soluble cytokines, especially interleukins plus TNF/CSF-family cytokines. Antibodies and receptors are not the target class; they are controlled perturbations used to ask how binding changes the cytokine's residue-level dynamics. This framing is more biologically coherent than treating every antibody–antigen complex as one class and is directly aligned with FlexPPI's outputs (RMSF, side-chain rotamer entropy and SASA).

## Live data inventory

The Harvard Dataverse API was queried on 2026-08-24 and returned ThermoPCD trajectory records for at least the following distinct cytokine systems: IL-1β–canakinumab (5BVP), IL-2–NARA1 (5LQB), IL-2–UFKA-20 (6YE3), IL-6–68F2 (4ZSF), IL-12–ustekinumab (3HMX), IL-16–14.1 (5FB8), IL-18–125-2H (2VXT), IL-23–7G10 (3D85), GM-CSF–autoantibodies (6BFS/6BFQ), TNF-α–therapeutic Fab systems (3WD5/4G3Y) and CXCL12/SDF-1–hu30D8 (4LMQ). ThermoPCD provides 100–500 ns trajectories at 310–313 K and, for selected systems, multiple random seeds and antigen-only simulations. This is therefore a family-level cohort rather than a single-case dataset.

## Biological question enabled by the class

The central question is: **do neutralizing partners inhibit cytokines only by covering receptor-binding residues, or also by reshaping the cytokine's internal flexibility at distal sites?** For each cytokine, compare predicted and MD-derived changes between apo and partner-bound states: `ΔRMSF = RMSF_bound - RMSF_apo`, with analogous signed changes for side-chain entropy and SASA. A negative value means a residue becomes more rigid or less exposed after binding; a positive value means it becomes more mobile or more exposed. Map these changes onto receptor interfaces, antibody epitopes, known mutational sites and conserved structural regions. This supports three falsifiable insights: conserved dynamic neutralization hotspots shared across cytokines; partner-specific allosteric sites that change far from the epitope; and temperature-sensitive loss or preservation of neutralization dynamics.

The literature makes this hypothesis biologically plausible rather than merely computational. IL-17 family measurements showed that higher cytokine flexibility correlates with higher receptor affinity and that an inhibitor can reduce affinity by rigidifying the cytokine without requiring a large static structural change. IL-2 work identified a helical-capping switch whose dynamics control partner affinity and selectivity. IL-6 MD showed that receptor binding rigidifies one part of the AB loop while increasing flexibility in another. These are precisely the types of residue-level signed responses FlexPPI is designed to predict.

## Recommended staged cohort

1. Start with GM-CSF because ThermoPCD exposes both antigen-only and antibody-complex trajectories; this is the cleanest paired response test.
2. Add IL-2 because two different antibodies bind the same cytokine, enabling partner-specific dynamic-response comparisons without changing the target protein family.
3. Add TNF-α for free versus therapeutic-antibody comparisons and multiple seeds, after careful sequence/construct mapping.
4. Use IL-16–14.1 as the mechanism-rich case study because its antibody-induced helix/groove rearrangement supplies named regions and residues for validation.
5. Expand to IL-1β, IL-6, IL-12, IL-18, IL-23 and CXCL12 once the paired-state analysis is stable.

Temperature points are perturbation conditions, not independent biological replicates; random seeds are the replicate unit. ThermoPCD metadata should be validated against RCSB chain identities, because 6BFS/6BFQ antibody names are inconsistent with their primary structural paper. Exact-PDB exclusion is also insufficient for a clean external test; sequence and interface-geometry similarity to FlexPPI training data must be audited.

# Broad protein-type target atlas: public MD data and biological insight potential

## Scope and decision rule

The search was broadened from cytokines to protein classes that satisfy three separate tests: a primary source must expose downloadable trajectories or a clearly documented trajectory-access route; there must be at least two biologically meaningful states to compare; and the decisive environmental factors must be represented by the present FlexPPI input. The last condition is important. FlexPPI currently consumes standard-amino-acid protein coordinates for a target and an optional protein partner, and predicts residue-level RMSF, side-chain rotamer entropy and SASA, including signed apo/holo responses. Small molecules, ATP, metal ions, lipids, DNA/RNA, glycans and most post-translational modifications enter PDB files as non-standard records and are not represented by the current parser. Consequently, a membrane receptor can have abundant MD data and still be a poor immediate target if the claimed mechanism depends on lipids, voltage or an ion. The catalogue below therefore distinguishes **data availability** from **model validity**.

## Ranked recommendation

The best immediate programme is not to select the protein class with the largest database, but to assemble four complementary, leakage-controlled test tracks. **Track 1, immune regulation**, combines checkpoint proteins and soluble cytokines, because multiple natural and therapeutic partners create clean protein-only contrasts and residue-level mechanisms can be checked against known epitopes and receptor sites. **Track 2, viral recognition**, uses RBD–ACE2 and RBD–antibody systems to test whether variants produce dynamic escape at residues that do not lose a direct static contact. **Track 3, general transient PPIs**, uses sequence-disjoint enzyme–inhibitor and signaling complexes to test conformational selection and induced fit across unrelated folds; this is the closest match to FlexPPI but requires a leakage-safe split because local DynaRepo data have already entered development. **Track 4, protein stability**, uses mdCATH 320 K trajectories as an external, non-PPI test of whether predicted local dynamics anticipate unfolding hotspots at elevated temperature. Together these tracks ask distinct biological questions and prevent success on one immunological family from being mistaken for a general dynamic principle.

Within the present implementation, the practical priority order is: (1) immune checkpoints, (2) soluble cytokines, (3) RBD/ACE2 and viral antibody complexes, (4) new transient soluble PPIs, (5) atomistic antibody–antigen complexes, (6) viral enzyme dimers, and (7) mdCATH protein domains. Kinases and Hsp90 are the best next-stage targets after adding explicit nucleotide/PTM/ion features. GPCRs, channels and transporters become compelling only after adding membrane, ligand and ion context. Nucleosomes require DNA/PTM/ion-aware inputs, and IDPs require an ensemble- or sequence-based architecture rather than merely adding another node type.

## How the model yields a biological insight rather than a prediction map

Every proposed result should come from a controlled contrast. For the same target sequence, calculate `ΔRMSF = RMSF_state2 − RMSF_state1`, `ΔHχ` and `ΔSASA`. A negative `ΔRMSF` is local rigidification; a positive value is local flexibilization. The meaningful question is not simply whether binding makes the whole protein more rigid, but **where mobility is redistributed**. A valid analysis maps the signed changes to the direct interface, catalytic or receptor-binding site, conserved family motifs, distal allosteric sites, disease/resistance mutations and experimentally measured functional residues. It then repeats the result across independent random seeds and related proteins, and checks that the pattern is not a consequence of temperature, force field, missing residues or training-set similarity.

This design supports eight recurring, falsifiable biological insights: interface-local rigidification versus distal allostery; pre-existing interface preorganization versus partner-induced motion; different partners leaving distinct dynamic fingerprints on the same target; transient exposure of cryptic pockets or epitopes; disease or resistance mutations acting dynamically despite retaining static contacts; local 310 K dynamics anticipating thermal unfolding; family-conserved dynamic hotspots after sequence/structure alignment; and compensatory flexibility, where one region rigidifies while another becomes more mobile. These are hypotheses generated by the model and corroborated by held-out MD; causal claims still require perturbation experiments such as mutagenesis, binding kinetics, HDX-MS, NMR relaxation or single-molecule measurements.

## Dataset-wide cautions

ATLAS and DynaRepo are already named as local FlexPPI data sources, so their systems are useful for development and mechanism mining but are not automatically independent validation. Excluding an exact PDB identifier is insufficient: sequence identity, target-chain overlap, interface geometry and homologous family leakage must also be audited. Temperature conditions are perturbations rather than biological replicates, while separately initiated random seeds are the replicate unit. Enhanced-sampling and coarse-grained trajectories require reweighting or target-specific labels; they should not be silently mixed with conventional all-atom RMSF, side-chain entropy and SASA. Finally, [MemProtMD](https://memprotmd.bioch.ox.ac.uk/home/) is valuable for prepared membrane systems and final embedded structures, but its 1 μs coarse-grained membrane-assembly trajectories are not generally downloadable and therefore were not counted as a label source here.

# 改善 FlexPPI 在 PPI 界面预测中过度刚化：机制导向文献调研与模型路线（2026-08-25）

## 结论先行

在“不引入新的外部训练数据类型”的约束下，FlexPPI 仍有相当明确的改进空间，而且最值得做的不是继续扩大 IPA 编码器，也不是再加入一个更复杂的静态距离函数，而是把现有 MD 轨迹重新标注成“动态界面监督”，再让模型学习何时应该采用界面刚化先验、何时应该关闭或减弱这个先验。当前 Zenodo 6638504 实验说明模型输出与伙伴距离的宏平均 Spearman 为 0.438，而真实 ΔRMSF 与伙伴距离为 0.433；与此同时，纯距离先验对 ΔRMSF 的 Spearman 为 0.433，高于模型的 0.335。这不是简单地说模型“太依赖距离”，而是说模型基本学会了界面效应的平均曲线，却没有学会平均曲线周围最重要的条件差异：一个起始结构中的接触可能在轨迹中持续存在，也可能间歇开合、在多个接触网络之间切换，或者伴随伙伴整体重定向；这些状态在同一个静态最短距离下可以产生完全不同的结合响应。

这里有一个必要条件：所谓“无需新数据”是指不增加新的复合物或实验模态，但仍能重新访问原训练轨迹或至少访问保存的多窗口/多帧中间量。若实际只剩模型 checkpoint 和逐残基 RMSF/χ/SASA 汇总标签，而原轨迹与帧级信息已不可用，就无法事后恢复接触持续率和界面亚态；那时只能采用静态 core/rim、ΔSASA、堆积、ANM/GNM、残差化与伙伴反事实等弱约束，预期上限也会更低。当前本地 Zenodo 与 DynaRepo 工作流保留轨迹，因此本报告按可重新标注的情形设计。

因此，本报告的首要建议是构建一个低容量、可解释的三状态门控响应模型，暂称 `FlexPPI-DynGate`。它不把“离伙伴近”直接等同于刚化，而是先预测每个残基属于稳定接触、动态重排或远端传播三种动力学状态的概率，再由该概率决定是否启用负向刚化先验。门控监督无需新实验数据，可以直接从模型原有训练轨迹计算：每个残基作为界面残基的持续率、跨链接触占有率、接触开关熵、界面接触指纹的亚态熵、伙伴相对平移/转动方差、侧链旋转异构状态熵，以及窗口间一致性。推理时不能直接获得这些 MD 量，所以它们只作为训练期辅助标签；真正的输入仍是可从单个 apo/holo 结构计算的距离、埋藏面积、core/rim/support 区域、局部堆积、空腔/间隙、氢键和盐桥几何、局部挫折度、弹性网络软模态等。这个训练/推理边界是整个方案能否成立的关键。

## 为什么“结合界面必然刚化”是不充分的先验

Grünberg、Nilges 与 Leckner 对 17 个复合物及游离组分的 MD 分析得到的核心结论不是“结合限制了整条蛋白的构象自由度”，而是结合会重新分配动力学；结合既可能造成不利的构象熵损失，也可能通过其他区域的运动变化补偿这一损失（[Structure 2006](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.str.2006.01.014)）。Tandon 等随后在 58 个有结合态和游离态结构的复合物上发现，55% 的体系在远离界面的区域存在显著动力学变化，并且约 90% 的体系发生残基间通信改变（[Structure 2021](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.str.2020.11.015)）。这解释了为什么“远端平均 ΔRMSF 约为零”并不能证明远端没有信号：正向和负向变化会在平均时抵消，而 FlexPPI 需要预测的是逐残基异质性。

界面本身也不是一个固定残基集合。MDcons 早已用随时间变化的跨链共识接触图描述界面（[BMC Bioinformatics 2014](https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2105-15-S5-S1)）；Zenodo 6638504 对应的 Martin 与 Frezza 工作直接分析了接触持续性、界面聚类和水介导接触，并指出 PPI 界面不能再被视为静态对象（[Frontiers in Molecular Biosciences 2022](https://doi.org/10.3389/fmolb.2022.970109)）。2026 年的 DynaPIN 将“残基在模拟中保持界面身份的持续率”作为核心动态界面定义，并发现这种界面柔性与传统 docking benchmark 的 rigid/medium/difficult 分类并不等价（[Journal of Molecular Biology 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmb.2026.169942)）。DynaBench 则为 Docking Benchmark 5.5 中 200 多个复合物提供三条 100 ns 轨迹，明确把动态界面描述用于模型训练和动态感知评估（[Journal of Molecular Biology 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmb.2026.169650)）。这些结果共同支持一个直接的建模判断：静态距离适合做低容量平均先验，但动态界面身份应决定这个先验对每个残基施加多大权重。

界面区域也应进一步区分 core、rim 和 support。已有结构统计显示，interface core 的组成和蛋白内部更相似，rim 更接近普通溶剂暴露表面，core 往往贡献大部分接触面积（[Journal of Molecular Biology 2010](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmb.2010.09.028)）。更早的 apo/holo 分析还发现，core 界面残基即使在伙伴不存在时也倾向于比普通表面更不易运动，而外围界面更易运动（[Journal of Molecular Biology 2005](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmb.2005.01.058)）。因此，同为 4 Å 跨链距离，一个本来就预组织、紧密埋藏的 core 接触和一个位于边缘、带有多种侧链构象的短暂接触，不应得到相同的负向 ΔRMSF。

## 可以表征界面柔性的量：按实际可用性分层

| 层级 | 量及直观含义 | 如何计算 | 在 FlexPPI 中的正确用法 | 优先级 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 现有轨迹辅助监督 | 残基界面持续率 `p_int(i)`：一个残基有多少比例的帧处于界面 | 每帧判断该残基是否存在小于阈值的跨链重原子接触，然后取时间平均 | 动态门控头的直接标签；推理时由静态结构预测，不能作为输入真值 | P0 |
| 现有轨迹辅助监督 | 边接触占有率 `p_ij`：某一对跨链残基实际接触了多久 | `p_ij = mean_t I[d_ij(t) < r_c]` | 增加跨链 edge head，监督接触是否真正稳定 | P0 |
| 现有轨迹辅助监督 | 接触开关熵：接触是否频繁出现和消失 | `h_ij = -p_ij log p_ij -(1-p_ij) log(1-p_ij)`；在相关边上聚合到残基 | 区分“持续接触”和“频繁开合”；避免单纯距离复制 | P0 |
| 现有轨迹辅助监督 | 界面亚态熵：整个接触网络在几种模式之间切换 | 对每帧跨链接触指纹聚类，按簇占比 `π_k` 计算 `-Σπ_k log π_k` | 复合物级或链侧级 gate 标签；直接针对 1AK4/3SGB 类重定向体系 | P0 |
| 现有轨迹辅助监督 | 伙伴相对位姿方差 | 对齐目标链后，记录伙伴质心平移和三维转动，再计算协方差 | 识别 rigid-body 重定向；作为全局 gate 与分层评测量 | P0 |
| 现有轨迹辅助监督 | 侧链 χ 状态分布和熵 | 把 χ 二面角分到旋转异构状态，计算状态概率和 Shannon 熵 | 与其只回归一个熵值，优先辅助预测状态占有率，再由概率计算熵 | P1 |
| 现有轨迹辅助监督 | 每残基 3×3 位移协方差 | 在局部残基坐标系中计算 Cα 位移的三维协方差 | 预测运动大小和方向；RMSF 为协方差迹的平方根 | P1 |
| 现有轨迹辅助监督 | 残基间动态耦合 | 从位移协方差、DCCM 或互信息得到成对耦合 | 为远端增柔/刚化专家提供监督；可先做低秩或邻域版本 | P1 |
| 静态可部署输入 | ΔSASA、埋藏面积、core/rim/support | 比较目标链单独结构和复合物中的 SASA；按结合后暴露程度分区 | 控制平均刚化先验强度，比单一最短距离更有物理意义 | P0 |
| 静态可部署输入 | 局部堆积、接触数、空腔和 gap volume index | 统计邻近重原子、Voronoi/空腔体积，或界面间隙体积除以界面面积 | 判断短距离接触是紧密预组织还是存在可重排空间 | P0/P1 |
| 静态可部署输入 | 氢键、盐桥、疏水接触和形状互补 | 从供受体、带电基团、非极性原子及表面法向计算 | 作为边类型；仍需由动态占有率监督，不能把几何满足直接当稳定 | P1 |
| 静态可部署输入 | 局部能量挫折度 | 把原生接触能量与突变或构象 decoy 接触比较 | 高挫折区域可能对应功能性切换/粗糙能量景观，适合做 gate 输入 | P1 |
| 静态可部署输入 | ANM/GNM 软模态、PRS 响应 | 从 Cα 弹性网络的 Hessian 低频特征向量和响应计算 | 提供单结构可获得的运动方向及远端机械耦合先验 | P1 |
| 昂贵、可后置 | 水桥占有率、界面水驻留和氢键重排时间 | 显式水轨迹中追踪同时接触两条链的水分子及驻留 | 可解释极性/带电界面，但不建议作为第一版模型的必要输入 | P2 |

这里最容易混淆的是“占有率高”和“熵低”。对某一条起始接触而言，`p≈1` 表示几乎一直存在，开关熵接近零；`p≈0.5` 表示不断开合，开关熵最高；`p≈0` 也会得到低开关熵，但这表示这条候选边几乎从未形成。因此模型必须同时预测 `p` 和 `h`，不能只用熵。残基界面持续率则把一个残基与所有伙伴边汇总，适合作为门控；边接触占有率保留“换了哪个接触对象”的信息，适合监督跨链图。

侧链构象熵有坚实的历史依据。Cole 与 Warwicker 用旋转异构概率估计界面侧链熵，发现多数真实界面在未结合状态下就比普通表面更不灵活，并指出侧链熵项可改善 PPI 关联计算（[Protein Science 2002](https://doi.org/10.1110/ps.0222702)）。NMR 序参量与构象熵之间也已建立残基类型相关的定量映射（[JACS 2009](https://doi.org/10.1021/ja902477s)）。不过，当前 FlexPPI 在 Zenodo 数据上绝对 χ 熵 Spearman 很高而 Δχ 响应很弱，说明问题未必是“没有 χ 信息”，更可能是把复杂的多状态概率压成单个熵标量后，再直接做 holo−apo 回归，丢掉了状态交换结构。因此建议把每个 χ 的三态占有率或联合 rotamer state logits 作为辅助头，由概率分布导出熵，而不是只增加 Δχ 损失权重。

水可以解释一部分“接触很近却没有明显刚化”的反例，但不应成为第一阶段的工程依赖。蛋白–蛋白界面水的氢键重排可比体相水慢约三个数量级（[Scientific Reports 2017](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-017-09466-w)），Martin 与 Frezza 的 8 个复合物也观察到稳定的水介导接触（[Frontiers 2022](https://doi.org/10.3389/fmolb.2022.970109)）。这说明静态结构中没有直接氢键并不等于相互作用不稳定，显式水可能屏蔽同号电荷并桥接两侧残基。但水驻留标签对帧率、溶剂模型和轨迹长度更敏感，计算与部署成本也更高，适合作为 P2 机制消融，而不是 P0 的核心修复。

## 推荐模型：FlexPPI-DynGate

当前 `FlexPPI` 用同一状态编码器分别处理 apo 与 holo，输出 RMSF、χ 熵和 SASA，再把两侧残基表示及状态预测差输入响应头。现有损失是标签方差感知的异方差负对数似然，加上响应与状态相减的一致性约束。该设计可以利用伙伴上下文，但没有显式阻止响应头把“跨链距离”当成几乎唯一的响应解释。因此建议保留现有状态编码器，先替换响应建模和监督，不要立即大改主干。

对每个残基 `i`，先用一个低容量、单调或平滑的几何函数得到平均刚化先验 `b_i = b(d_i, ΔSASA_i, core/rim_i, packing_i)`；为了便于解释，可以限制 `b_i≤0`，并让它在远离界面时自然趋近零。随后由门控头输出三个概率 `q_i^stable`、`q_i^switch`、`q_i^remote`，三者相加为 1。最终响应不应是无条件的 `b_i + residual_i`，而建议写成：

`ΔRMSF_hat_i = q_i^stable · b_i + q_i^switch · r_i^switch + q_i^remote · r_i^remote`。

这里 `stable` 专家表示接触持续且界面亚态少，允许使用负向刚化先验；`switch` 专家表示界面身份或接触对象频繁切换，其输出不限制正负；`remote` 专家通过目标链内部的耦合图传播伙伴扰动，同样不限制正负。直观地说，两个残基即使都离伙伴 4 Å，只要第一个被预测为稳定 core 接触，`q_stable` 就大，距离先验会生效；第二个位于高熵 rim、接触频繁更换，`q_switch` 就大，模型可以预测接近零甚至增柔。门控头用静态表示推理，但训练期由 `p_int`、接触开关熵、界面簇数和相对位姿方差监督。这样既保留数据中非常强的真实平均刚化规律，又给模型一个有监督的“关闭刚化先验”机制。

`remote` 专家不宜只读取跨链最短距离，而应读取目标蛋白内部的动态耦合表示。最低成本版本可把 ANM/GNM 低频模态投影或 perturbation-response scanning 结果加入 pair features；更强版本从现有 MD 轨迹监督一个低秩残基耦合矩阵。DynaProt 的结果表明，标量 RMSF只表示运动幅度，会丢掉方向和残基间协方差；它直接预测每个残基的 3×3 位移协方差及 N×N 耦合，并能从协方差的迹恢复 RMSF（[DynaProt 2025/2026](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12425013/)）。FlexPPI 与它同样使用 IPA 局部坐标框架，因此增加一个正定协方差读出头在架构上是自然扩展：模型输出下三角矩阵 `L_i`，令 `Σ_i=L_iL_i^T+εI` 保证协方差合法，再用 `sqrt(trace(Σ_i))` 作为 RMSF。这样不仅可预测“晃动多大”，还可判断运动方向是朝界面法向、沿界面切向，还是沿铰链方向；这对伙伴重定向和滑移界面尤其重要。

## 损失函数：避免“反距离”过度纠偏

不建议简单加入一个惩罚项强迫预测与距离不相关，因为真实标签本身确实与距离相关；这样会把正确生物学先验一起删除。更稳妥的是先在训练复合物上拟合低容量几何基线 `b_i`，然后让动态专家学习残差 `r_i = ΔRMSF_i - b_i`。评估时不仅报告最终 ΔRMSF，还单独报告残差相对于几何基线的增益。几何基线必须在每个训练折内部拟合，不能用留出复合物的信息。

推荐总损失为：`L = L_response + λ_state L_state + λ_gate L_gate + λ_contact L_contact + λ_cov L_cov + λ_cal L_cal + λ_cons L_cons`。其中 `L_response` 继续使用标签方差感知的 Huber 或 Student-t 负对数似然，以降低少数极大 N/C 端涨落对回归的支配；`L_gate` 监督稳定/切换/远端三状态或软概率；`L_contact` 回归接触占有率并分类动态边；`L_cov` 比较预测和轨迹协方差；`L_cal` 匹配每个复合物、每个距离区间的预测均值、方差和负变化比例；`L_cons` 保留当前响应与状态相减的一致性，但不应让这个一致性压过直接响应标签。

现有窗口方差还可以形成一个“有证据的中性区”。令 `s_i=sqrt(var_apo_i+var_holo_i+τ²)`，只有当 `|Δ_i|/s_i` 超过预设阈值时才对刚化/增柔方向施加强分类损失；其余残基标成“当前轨迹无法可靠区分于零”，只做软回归。这会阻止大量本来接近零且噪声较高的界面残基被模型统一推向负值。异方差头必须保留，但要监测方差膨胀，避免模型通过输出极大不确定性掩盖系统偏差。

`L_cal` 最好用分箱而不是单个相关系数。可以按 0–5 Å、5–8 Å、8–12 Å、>12 Å，再按 core/rim 或高低接触熵细分，匹配每个复合物中 `mean(Δ_hat)`、`std(Δ_hat)` 和 `P(Δ_hat<-0.1)`。这相当于告诉模型：界面整体可以明显刚化，但不能通过把每个残基都预测成同一个负值来获得低损失。复合物必须等权，否则大蛋白会在损失中淹没小蛋白。

## 不增加新数据时可做的同轨迹自监督与反事实训练

第一类是帧级增强：同一轨迹抽取多个结构帧，但只把它们当作同一状态的多视图，让不同帧得到一致的宏观状态预测，同时允许局部接触头随帧改变。这样可以迫使编码器区分“状态不变”和“接触瞬时变化”。若推理阶段只有单个结构，训练时不能把轨迹统计量直接拼到输入；可采用随机单帧训练、跨帧一致性和集合均值蒸馏，让单结构学生去逼近多帧教师。

第二类是伙伴反事实。把伙伴整体平移到接触范围外，显式伙伴上下文贡献应趋近零；把非天然伙伴或旋转后的伙伴放在同一空间邻域，模型不应只因距离相近就复现天然响应。不过，这类反事实不能粗暴规定总 ΔRMSF 必为零，因为 holo 目标链坐标本身可能已经包含结合诱导构象。更安全的做法是把响应分成“目标链 apo→holo 几何项”和“伙伴身份/接触项”，只约束后者在移远伙伴时消失，并用天然/交换伙伴的对比损失学习接触特异性。

第三类是任务遮盖：随机遮掉一部分跨链边，让模型根据界面其余部分恢复接触占有率或边类型；随机遮掉局部残基，让模型恢复其动态类别。这些标签全部来自同一批轨迹，不引入外部数据，同时能减少响应头直接记忆距离函数的捷径。

## 与当前 8 个复合物结果相连的可证伪假设

| 假设 | 现有证据 | 新模型应给出的可证伪预测 |
|---|---|---|
| 稳定界面的刚化主要由持续接触解释 | 1GCQ/2OOB 的 ΔRMSF Spearman 平均 0.605，显著高于重定向体系 | 这两者应有更高 `p_int`、更低界面亚态熵和更高 `q_stable`；加入 gate 后性能不应下降 |
| 重定向界面的失败来自静态距离不能表示接触切换 | 1AK4/3SGB 平均 Spearman 0.173 | 两者应有更高位姿方差和接触开关熵；DynGate 应主要改善这一组而不是只改善总体平均 |

这 8 个复合物不应直接用于反复调参后再宣称独立验证。五个 PDB 已在状态预训练中有精确身份重叠；三个 exact-PDB-unseen 也不足以支持高功效结论。更安全的用法是：用它们做机制审计和预注册消融，所有阈值和特征在训练集内确定；报告全 8 个的 leave-one-complex-out 结果，同时把 1BRS、2OOB、3SGB 作为探索性未见集合。若以后使用 DynaBench 等外部轨迹，应先做序列/结构同源簇拆分。

## 具体实验顺序与停止规则

**阶段 A：只做标签与诊断，不改模型。** 从现有训练轨迹及 Zenodo 轨迹计算 `p_int`、`p_ij`、`h_ij`、界面接触簇、目标对齐后的伙伴位姿方差、core/rim/support、ΔSASA 和局部堆积。先检验这些量是否能在复合物级解释当前模型残差，特别是 `error = Δ_hat - Δ_true` 是否在低持续率、高接触熵和高位姿方差区域更偏负。如果这些描述符连当前误差都不能分层，就不应马上加入神经网络。

**阶段 B：最低成本修复。** 在冻结或低学习率主干上加入几何基线残差化、距离分箱校准和显著性三分类头，不增加动态 gate。成功标准不是总体 Spearman 微升，而是界面偏差、负比例校准和正向异常召回改善，同时不损害 1GCQ/2OOB。

**阶段 C：核心 DynGate。** 加入 per-residue `p_int`/contact entropy 辅助头与三专家门控，先不做完整 N×N 协方差。必须做 `current → residual only → +p_int → +contact entropy → +global state gate` 逐步消融。若 `+p_int` 无法在留出复合物上降低相对距离基线的残差误差，则停止扩展，检查标签定义或静态可预测性。

**阶段 D：方向和远端耦合。** 加 3×3 局部协方差头，再加低秩链内耦合头。先用同一轨迹的 block split 检查协方差标签收敛；若不同时间块之间的协方差相似度很低，说明 300–500 ns 轨迹不足以给出可靠监督，不应把噪声塞进主模型。

**阶段 E：高成本机制项。** 最后才加入 local frustration、水桥占有率和界面水驻留。它们应被视为解释特定反例的增量信息，而不是默认必需特征。

建议主要指标包括：逐复合物宏平均 ΔRMSF Spearman 与 MAE；相对纯距离基线的残差 Spearman/MAE；0–5、5–12、>12 Å 的预测偏差；每层负变化比例校准；三状态 Brier score 或 ECE；真实界面增柔残基的 recall；远端显著正变化 top-k precision；稳定、多亚态、重定向三组性能；预测区间覆盖率。要预先规定“总体性能提高但界面负偏差更严重”不算成功。

## 最小可行版本

如果只允许做一版低风险改动，建议先实现四个标签和一个门控：`p_int(i)`、残基聚合接触开关熵、复合物界面亚态熵、伙伴相对位姿方差；静态输入补上 ΔSASA、core/rim/support 和局部堆积；响应写成 `q_stable·b_distance + (1-q_stable)·r_free`。这比一次性加入水、自由能、完整协方差和大规模 ensemble 更容易验证因果链：若它能特异性改善 1AK4/3SGB 和高接触熵残基，同时保持 1GCQ/2OOB，就说明“过度刚化来自缺少动态门控”的假设得到支持；若不能，再考虑协方差与链内耦合，而不是盲目增加主干容量。

# FlexPPI 背景、引言与图片叙事调研（2026-08-30）

## Final report

FlexPPI 最稳妥的定位是“从静态多链复合物预测伙伴条件化的逐残基动态状态”，而不是 PPI 界面分类器或构象集合生成器。输入是目标链静态结构及可选伙伴环境，输出 RMSF、侧链转子占据 Shannon 熵 $H_\chi$ 和 SASA 等互补的绝对状态；只有拥有独立 apo/holo 结构或轨迹时，$\Delta y=y_{holo}-y_{apo}$ 才可称真实配对响应。若保持结合态目标坐标不变、仅删除伙伴，则只能称 fixed-coordinate partner ablation 或 partner-context response，不能称 binding-induced conformational response。建议使用的英文定位是：**FlexPPI is a partner-aware, rigid-motion-invariant, multi-task predictor of residue-wise dynamic states from static protein complexes, coupled with paired and controlled-context evaluations of binding-associated response.** 若现阶段主要证据是删链实验，则末尾应改为 **controlled evaluations of partner-context sensitivity**。

背景按六段推进：第一段用“相近平均结构仍可有不同局部波动、侧链转子和暴露”建立静态结构与构象集合之间的矛盾；第二段说明 NMR/重复 MD 丰富但昂贵，ATLAS、DynaRepo 等标准化轨迹库使监督学习可行；第三段把相关方法分为物理近似、紧凑描述符直接回归和构象集合生成三路；第四段引入 PeSTo/DeepInteract 一类多链几何模型并指出界面概率不等于伙伴条件化动态；第五段严格定义 absolute state、true paired response 和 fixed-coordinate context ablation；第六段才介绍共享 fixed-frame IPA 编码器、多描述符状态头、配对响应头、状态差一致性和 noise-aware uncertainty，并用“整体改善但局部可能过度刚化”的结果说明为什么不能只报告整体 RMSF 相关性。

Figure 1 应是 **Motivated Example / running example + failure case**，而不是当前预训练网络图。建议三栏：(a) 同一目标的 apo 与 holo/complex，结构旁并列 RMSF、$H_\chi$、SASA profile，标出一处刚化和一处增柔，表达 binding redistributes rather than uniformly suppresses dynamics；(b) partner-agnostic predictor 只能输出通用 profile，标出 no partner context / no signed response；(c) FlexPPI 的 apo 与 holo 两路进入 shared $\theta$ 几何编码器，输出 $\hat y_{apo}$、$\hat y_{holo}$、$\Delta\hat y$ 和 uncertainty，右侧以 overall/interface/peptide/partner 分区展示成功与 over-rigidification failure。真实 apo/holo 用实线，固定坐标删伙伴用虚线。图注首句建议为：**FlexPPI separates absolute residue dynamics from partner-context response, enabling direct tests of where a static multichain model succeeds and fails.**

最值得参考的图是 AlphaFlow Fig. 1 的单一主机制与一致蛋白配色、BackFlip/FliPS Fig. 1 的“结构 + profile + MD validation”闭环、Flexpert Fig. 1 的嵌套/共享编码器、RMSF-net Fig. 1 对训练期 MD 标签和推理输入的分离、PeSTo Fig. 1 的几何架构与 residue-colored output 并列。DynaProt 的表达力–效率任务地图只应在横纵轴有可操作定义时使用。诱导契合/构象选择漏斗不适合作为 FlexPPI 的中央图，因为模型不预测路径、速率或自由能景观。

当前 `paper/figures/f1_pipeline.pdf` 是清楚的预训练流程图，但没有伙伴条件或配对响应，适合 Figure 2 而不适合全篇 Figure 1；这一缺失属于 MAJOR 叙事问题。`fig_flexppi_architecture.png` 和 `fig_flexppi_pretraining.png` 技术信息密度过高；`fig_flexppi_design_pipeline.png` 适合未来应用/愿景，但当前若无完整设计闭环不应放大为已验证主贡献；`fig_itrace_main.png` 可作为结果主图，但应增加 interface/peptide/partner 分区和响应方向。
